XoFitLg Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Developer: XoFitLg

Information stored on your device

XoFitLg stores exercise names, muscle groups, equipment, notes, selected reference photos, training plans, workout dates, set results, workout duration, feelings, and preferences locally on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a service that receives or stores your training notebook.

Collection and tracking

XoFitLg does not automatically upload your training data or collect account details, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The app contains no ads or tracking tools, and we do not sell or rent your training data. Apple may process device backups or diagnostic information according to your device settings and its own privacy policy.

Photos and notifications

The app imports only the reference photos you choose in the system photo picker. If you choose Save Image when sharing a workout summary, iOS may request permission to add that image to Photos. Notification permission is requested only when you enable rest reminders. You can change these permissions in iPhone Settings. Workout recording remains available if you decline optional permissions. The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, or Health data.

Backups, exports, and sharing

You decide when to export a full backup, create a CSV file, or share a workout summary image, and which destination or recipient to use. Backups can include reference photos and all notebook records. Exported files are not encrypted by XoFitLg. Files you save to a cloud provider or share with another service are handled under that provider's policies. iOS device backups may include app data according to your settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or delete them, replace the notebook by restoring a backup, clear all data in Settings, or remove the app and its local data. Exercise archiving keeps past records. Clearing app data does not delete copies you previously exported, shared, or saved in device backups; manage those copies separately with the relevant provider. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your notebook.

Email support

If you email XoFitLg, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and is retained only as needed to handle the conversation and any applicable legal obligations. Your email provider also handles the message under its own policies. Avoid sending sensitive training information unless it is needed for your request.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. The current policy and its last updated date are available on this page. For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact the developer using the email address below.
