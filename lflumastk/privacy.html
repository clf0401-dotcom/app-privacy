1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how LfLumaStk handles information when you use the LfLumaStk iPhone app. The developer is LfLumaStk. Contact us at wwyy889911@outlook.com. This policy covers this version of the app and does not replace the policies of Apple, your email provider, or services you choose to use outside the app.

2. Information Stored on Your iPhone

The app stores projects, imported photos, videos, audio, fonts, editing settings, captions, exports, recovery points, and preview files on your iPhone. Local accounts store a username, an optional display name, protected password-verification data, and sign-in status. Passwords are not saved as readable text. Each account has its own local workspace, coin balance, purchase activity, and tool unlocks. Guest work is kept separately.

LfLumaStk does not operate a developer-hosted account database, media-upload service, or cloud synchronization service for this version. We do not receive your device-only passwords or project files automatically through the app.

3. Sign in with Apple and Purchases

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an app-specific user identifier, your name, and your email address or a private relay address, depending on your choices and what Apple returns. The app keeps available profile information locally to recognize your workspace. Signing in does not upload your projects or synchronize your wallet.

Apple processes in-app payments. The app uses verified purchase information and a local account association to assign coins and handle purchase updates or refunds. We do not receive your payment card number through the app. Your Apple sign-in identity and App Store payment account are separate. Apple handles information through its services under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Permissions and On-Device Processing

Photos and Files access lets you import media you select or save exports you request. Camera and microphone access supports capture and voiceovers. Optional speech recognition is configured to run on the device when the device and language support it; the app does not send audio to a developer-run transcription service. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can use manual captions or import subtitles.

Optional image analysis processes selected media on your iPhone for tools such as subject masks and suggested privacy regions. These tools can make mistakes; review the result before sharing. The app does not build a remote face-identification database. Face ID or device authentication for the workspace lock is handled by iOS; the app receives an authentication result, not your biometric templates.

The app does not request Contacts or current GPS location access. Imported files may already contain metadata, including capture dates or embedded location information. Original files and archives you share may retain that metadata. Review files before sharing them. You can change permissions in iOS Settings; denying a permission may disable the related feature, while other available editing tools remain usable.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Sharing

This version includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

When you choose an export, share action, external link, or file provider, the destination you choose may receive or process the selected content. iOS backups, iCloud Photos, and Files providers may store or transfer copies according to your settings. Apple may process diagnostics or service information according to your device settings and Apple's policies. These system services do not create an LfLumaStk cloud account or guarantee that local accounts and wallets can be recovered.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local information remains until you remove it, delete the related account, clear guest data, or it is removed by app or system cleanup. Projects can remain in Recently Deleted until you permanently delete them or the app's 30-day cleanup runs. Recovery copies or media still referenced by other projects may remain. Clearing preview cache does not delete original projects or media.

Use Profile > Delete Account to remove the signed-in local profile and its workspace, or Clear Guest Workspace for guest data. Deleting an account does not remove originals in Photos, copies you exported, or files you shared elsewhere. A limited record of purchase identifiers and account associations may remain in this iPhone's secure storage to prevent duplicate coin delivery and handle purchase updates. It is not used to recreate a deleted profile. System-managed secure records may outlast app removal.

Deleting an Apple-linked local account does not automatically revoke Apple authorization. Follow the app's instructions to stop using Sign in with Apple for LfLumaStk in your Apple Account settings or at account.apple.com. The app also responds to supported credential-state changes. We cannot remotely access, reset, or restore device-only passwords, accounts, projects, or wallets. Do not assume that reinstalling the app or restoring a device backup will recover them.
