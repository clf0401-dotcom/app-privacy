HandMcst Privacy Policy

Developer: HandMcst

Contact: fermlnpognantb@outlook.com

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Information you enter

Projects, material costs, labor details, expenses, quotes, customer names and contact details, notes, and business settings are stored locally on your iPhone. The app uses this information to calculate costs and prices, manage quotes, and create PDFs. HandMcst does not operate a server that receives this app data.

2. No automatic collection or tracking

HandMcst does not automatically collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your information. No account or internet connection is required for its core features.

3. Sharing and device backups

Quote PDFs are created on your device. You choose whether to export or share them using the iOS share sheet. The app or service you select handles the exported file under its own privacy policy. Device backups, if enabled, may include app data and are managed by Apple under your device settings. HandMcst does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service.

4. Retention and deletion

App records remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual projects and quotes, or use Settings > Erase All Data to clear projects, quotes, and saved settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Exported PDFs and device backups are separate copies; manage them in the destination app, service, or device backup settings. Exported PDFs cannot restore projects or quotes in HandMcst. Temporary PDF files used for sharing may remain in the app’s temporary storage until iOS clears them or the app is deleted.

5. Contact and support

If you contact HandMcst by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle it or meet applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by the email services used to send and receive it. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact email above. We cannot access or delete records stored only on your device.

6. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The current policy and its last updated date are available on this page. For privacy questions, contact HandMcst using the email above.
