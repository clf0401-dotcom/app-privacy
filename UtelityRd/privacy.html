UtelityRd Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Developer: UtelityRd

UtelityRd is developed and operated by UtelityRd. This Privacy Policy explains how the iPhone app handles information.

Information stored on your iPhone

Meter names, locations, types, units, reading dates, cumulative readings, unit prices, notes, and reading schedules are stored in the app on your iPhone. The app uses these records to calculate usage, display history and trends, estimate costs, and create reports. No account is required.

Automatic collection and tracking

UtelityRd does not automatically send your meter records, contact details, location, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics to the developer. The app has no ads or tracking tools and does not use your records for advertising. UtelityRd does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization.

Device permissions

UtelityRd does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, or photo library. If you choose another app or service from the iOS share sheet, that destination may request its own permissions.

Exports and sharing

CSV files of your readings and PNG images of monthly reports are created only when you request an export. Export files are prepared in the app’s temporary storage for the iOS share sheet. If you save or share a file, the destination handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Erasing records in UtelityRd does not remove exported copies.

Device backups

App data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your iOS backup settings. UtelityRd does not operate that backup service or access your backup contents. Backups are managed through iOS and the relevant service provider.

Retention and deletion

Saved meter records remain in the app until you delete them. You can delete individual readings, delete a meter together with its readings, or use Erase all data in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Exported files, backup copies, and correspondence you send separately may remain outside the app and must be managed separately.

Email support

If you choose to contact UtelityRd by email, your email address, message, and any attachments you provide are transmitted by your email service and received by UtelityRd to respond to your request. The app does not automatically attach or send your meter records. Please include only the information needed for your request.

Policy updates

Updates to this policy will appear in the policy available in the app with a revised effective date.
