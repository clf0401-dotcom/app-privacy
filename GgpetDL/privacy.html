GgpetDL Privacy Policy

Developer: GgpetDL

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Contact email: biyihan226@outlook.com

Your pet's life stays on your iPhone. Here's how your information is handled.

1. Who provides the app

GgpetDL is provided by GgpetDL ("we" or "us"). This policy explains how this version handles information when you use its pet profiles, care records, reminders, care cards, and backup tools. You do not need to register or sign in.

2. Information you choose to enter

The app processes pet names, types, photos, and optional profile details such as birthdays, homecoming dates, sex, and breed. You may also enter food and portions, routines, preferences, care times, weight entries, milestone tags, photos, and notes. Contact details for a care card are entered manually and included only if you enable that section. Please avoid entering information you do not have permission to use.

3. Local use and storage

Your saved profiles, records, selected photos, and reminder rules are stored in the app's local storage on this iPhone. We do not operate an app backend or automatically upload this information to the developer. The app uses it locally to display your journal, calculate last-care times and weight charts, schedule reminders, and create exports. Care-card edits are used to prepare the card; they do not automatically replace your saved daily habits.

4. Permissions you control

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker gives the app the images you select without granting broad access to your library. Selected images are resized for local storage. Notification permission is requested when you save your first reminder. Declining these permissions does not prevent other local journaling features. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. The app does not request location, microphone, contacts, or tracking access.

5. No advertising or tracking

This version contains no advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, or developer-operated cloud services. We do not sell your information or send journal entries, photos, or device identifiers to advertising partners. Apple may handle App Store, device backup, or diagnostics information under its own privacy settings and policies; those system services are separate from the app.

6. Exports and sharing

Backup JSON files can include all saved pets, care records, reminder rules, and optionally photos. CSV exports contain the chosen pets' care records, names, timestamps, notes, and photo counts, but not the photos themselves, full profiles, or reminder rules. CSV exports cannot be restored into the app. Care cards contain only the sections you select, along with the preparation time. Nothing is shared automatically. You choose a destination in the system share sheet or Files picker. Any app, person, or storage provider you choose may receive that exported information and applies its own policies. The developer does not receive a copy through this process. Anyone with access to a backup file may be able to read its contents.

7. Retention and deletion

Active journal data stays on this device until you remove it or the app's storage is removed. You can edit or delete individual records, delete a pet and its associated records and reminders in Profile → Your data, and export a backup before deletion. Restoring a backup creates new profiles and preserves existing ones. Removing the app may remove its local data. Exported files, recipient copies, and operating-system backups are not automatically deleted when you delete a record, profile, or the app; manage those copies separately.

8. Security and your choices

The app uses iOS app storage protections and writes saved journal changes atomically. No storage or backup method can guarantee protection against every loss or unauthorized access. Exported backup files are not password-encrypted by this app. Protect your iPhone and choose secure locations for backups and care cards. Because we do not hold an online copy of your journal, we cannot retrieve it for you or make changes to your local data remotely.

9. Children and policy updates

GgpetDL is a general pet care journal and is not specifically directed to children. It does not knowingly collect journal information from children through an online service. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use and sharing. If the app's privacy practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated with the relevant release. New permissions or consent will be requested where required.

10. Questions

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information below. Do not include private journal exports or photos unless you intend to share them.
