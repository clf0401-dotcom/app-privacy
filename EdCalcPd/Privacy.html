About this policy

EdCalcPd is developed and operated by EdCalcPd. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its calculation sheets, budgets, and snapshots.

Information the app does not collect

EdCalcPd works offline and does not require an account. The app does not send your calculation entries, device identifiers, location, or usage activity to the developer. It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools. We do not sell your calculation data or use it for advertising.

Information saved on your iPhone

The app saves sheet names, labels, expressions, quantities, unit prices, budgets, selected lines, line order, snapshots, modification times, and the last opened sheet on your iPhone. This information is used to calculate results and restore your work when you reopen the app. A local recovery copy of the saved sheets is also maintained. The developer cannot access these files through the app.

Device backups and Apple services

EdCalcPd does not provide its own cloud storage or synchronization service. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup. Those backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple, are handled by Apple or your backup provider under their policies and settings.

Permissions

The calculation features do not require access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. EdCalcPd does not request tracking permission. If you choose to copy text or open an email link, that action is handled through the relevant iOS feature or app.

If you contact the developer

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address you send from, your message, and any attachments you include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and keep it only as long as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Your calculation sheets are not automatically attached. Email providers process messages under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it in the app or delete the app from your iPhone. Deleting a line from a sheet does not delete copies of that line in saved snapshots; delete the relevant snapshots or the entire sheet to remove those copies. Deleting a sheet also removes its saved snapshots from the app. Offloading the app may keep its documents and data. Device backups are managed separately through your device or backup settings. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local sheets.

Security

The app uses the storage protections and app sandbox provided by iOS. No method of storing or communicating information can be guaranteed to be completely secure. EdCalcPd does not operate a server that stores your calculation sheets.

Children

EdCalcPd is a general-purpose calculation tool and does not request personal information from children. If you have a question about a child's privacy, contact the developer using the email below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. The current policy is available in the app under About > Privacy Policy.
