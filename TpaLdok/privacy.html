TpaLdok Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

About this policy

TpaLdok is an offline packing planner developed by TpaLdok. This policy explains how the app handles the information you enter and the files you choose to use.

Information stored on your device

Trip details, traveler names, packing lists, quantities, weights, tasks, templates, and notes are saved in the app’s local storage. This information is not automatically sent to TpaLdok or to a developer-operated server.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or an app-operated cloud sync service. TpaLdok does not automatically collect or transmit your personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers. Its planning features work without an internet connection.

Permissions and file access

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photo library, camera, or microphone. When you import a backup, the system file picker lets you choose a file for the app to read.

Files you export or restore

Exported backups and packing checklists can contain the information you entered, including traveler names and trip notes. You control where those files are saved and who can access them. Restoring a valid backup replaces current app data only after your confirmation. If you choose a cloud storage or sharing service in system tools, that provider handles the selected files under its own terms and privacy policy. Choose On My iPhone for local file storage.

Device backups

Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by the operating system and the services you enable. TpaLdok does not operate a remote backup or data recovery service.

Keeping and deleting your data

Information stays in local app storage until you delete it, reset the app’s data, or remove the app, subject to any backups you retain. Reset All Data removes your trips and custom content and restores the starter templates and item library. Removing the app removes its local storage. Exported files and operating system backups must be managed or deleted separately.

Policy updates

If the app’s data practices change, an updated policy will be included in a future release with a revised date. You can read this policy in Settings without an internet connection.
